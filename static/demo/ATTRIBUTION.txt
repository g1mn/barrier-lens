Two clips are the first 10 s of Pexels videos (Pexels License: free to use and modify), normalised to 10 fps and
1280x720 (or 720x1280), faces blurred on our server (OpenCV YuNet) before any model saw them. Licence plates are not
detected automatically (no plate detector); none are readable in these clips at this size (checked by eye 10-29).

scooters-city-live.mp4 — "Urban Electric Scooters Parked in Cityscape" by FABIO REIS DE ABREU (PORTUGA),
  https://www.pexels.com/video/urban-electric-scooters-parked-in-cityscape-36383602/
sidewalk-closed-live.mp4 — "Sidewalk Closed Due to Construction" by BJ Zurc,
  https://www.pexels.com/video/sidewalk-closed-due-to-construction-35845191/
Not in this folder, credited anyway: "A Row of Electric Scooters Parked on the Sidewalk" by Alberto Escalona,
  https://www.pexels.com/video/a-row-of-electric-scooters-parked-on-the-sidewalk-15421497/ — the clip uploaded in the
  demo video's live run, and the source of the SAM test fixtures in data/fixtures/sam/scooters-row-* (masks only, no image data).
None of these Pexels clips was filmed in Seoul; a pin a sample drops on the map is illustrative.

sanpo-corridor-live.mp4 — from the SANPO dataset by Google Research, CC BY 4.0
  (https://github.com/google-research-datasets/sanpo_dataset, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
  Session Xoe7Kec_tA-bzqYd6hDEMMF6-rFrZx1b, chest camera, left lens, frames 0-149 (10 s at 15 fps).
  Changes: frames joined into a video, scaled from 2208x1242 to 1280x720, normalised to 10 fps, faces blurred again by
  our server (the dataset already blurs faces and plates), measurement overlays drawn by the app. The camera height (1.16 m) and tilt (12.1 deg down) used for
  this run come from the dataset's own stereo depth maps (a ground-plane fit; height from frames 300-450 of the same
  session, tilt and roll averaged over frames 0-149). The depth maps are themselves estimates (CREStereo), not survey
  measurements. Method and the stereo cross-check: docs/sanpo-check.md.

intro-corridor.mp4, intro-corridor-77.jpg — the opening intro only, from the same SANPO session (CC BY 4.0).
  The clip is source frames 71-116 (the 3 s that end on the measured frame), scaled to 1280x720, faces blurred by our
  server (OpenCV YuNet), interpolated from 15 to 30 fps (ffmpeg minterpolate). The still is frame 77 of
  sanpo-corridor-live.mp4, the frame the recorded run measured. intro-corridor.json holds fields copied from
  sanpo-corridor-live.json by scripts/build_intro.py (outlines, gap, band, verdict, explanation).

The .json next to each clip is the response of a live analyze run on it (2026-10-29, Muse Spark 1.3 + SAM 3.1).
