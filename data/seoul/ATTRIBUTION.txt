towing-yangjae.json
  Seoul Open Data Plaza, 서울시 전동킥보드 견인 현황 (OA-23057, service aiTbAutoKickboard).
  Licence: Korea Open Government License Type 1 (공공누리 제1유형: 출처표시).
  Source: 서울특별시, https://data.seoul.go.kr/dataList/OA-23057/S/1/datasetView.do
  Changes: kept rows whose address is in 서울특별시 서초구 양재동, grouped by lot address, counted by
  report date and form category (free-text categories folded into one bucket), and placed in 300 / 500 /
  1000 m distance bands from the centre by geocoding each lot address with the VWorld address API
  (국토교통부 브이월드, api.vworld.kr) at build time. VWorld results must be used in real time and may not be
  stored, so no coordinates are kept: only the band.
